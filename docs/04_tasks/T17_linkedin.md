## Entwurf
Built solo at the Hack Nation Global AI Hackathon, Munich hub: AfyaNote 🩺📵
A tiny model (243 KB) that runs offline in the phone browser and turns a short Swahili visit note into a reviewed referral draft for community health promoters. It shows where every suggestion comes from, marks what was denied or about someone else, and never decides for the health worker.
Small AI, checkable line by line. Thanks to @Hack Nation and the World Bank for the challenge.
